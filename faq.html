Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Online Registration & Applying

To apply for any advertised project, visit portal.nts.org.pk. Create a one-time profile using your CNIC, mobile number, and email. Complete your biographical and educational records, select your test project from Open Applications, and generate your 1Link 1Bill fee challan.

You can edit your personal details and qualifications on the portal before the project closing deadline. After the last date of submission, no changes can be made.

Fee Deposit via 1Link 1Bill

Open EasyPaisa or JazzCash → tap Bill Payment → select 1Bill / Others → enter your 1Bill Consumer Number generated on your NTS slip → verify candidate name and amount → click Pay. Your payment is verified automatically within 24 hours.

No! Because 1Link 1Bill payments are computerized, your payment status updates electronically on your portal dashboard. You do not need to courier the deposit slip unless explicitly instructed in the project advertisement.

Roll Number Slips & Examination Center

Roll Number Slips are uploaded on the website 7 to 10 days before the examination date. Candidates also receive an SMS alert on their registered mobile number.

If your photo does not appear, paste one recent passport-sized photograph attested with glue on the designated photo box on the printed slip and bring your original CNIC to the test center.

Mobile phones, smart watches, digital cameras, calculators, hand bags, books, and weapons are strictly disallowed. Center management does not take custody of prohibited items.

Results, Score Validity & Rechecking

NAT™ test scores are valid for one full year (12 months). GAT™ General and GAT™ Subject scores remain valid for two full years (24 months) from the announcement date.

Download the Paper Rechecking Form from the Downloads page, deposit the rechecking fee of Rs. 500/- via 1Bill, and submit the form within 15 days of result declaration.
