National Aptitude Test (NAT™)

The National Aptitude Test (NAT™) is Pakistan’s premier standardized test designed for admissions to undergraduate degree programs in higher education institutions. Over 40 leading public and private Pakistani universities accept NAT™ scores for their bachelor's admissions.

Key Features:
  • Conducted every calendar month throughout the year nationwide.
  • Total Questions: 90 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) | Time Allowed: 120 Minutes.
  • Score Validity: One Full Year (12 Months) from the date of test.
  • Single test score can be submitted to multiple member universities.

Graduate Assessment Test (GAT™ - General)

The GAT™ - General is designed for candidates seeking admission to MS / M.Phil programs and for applicants seeking award of HEC Indigenous & Overseas scholarships.

Key Features:
  • Conducted bi-monthly (6 times a year) across Pakistan.
  • Total Questions: 100 MCQs | Time Allowed: 120 Minutes.
  • Score Validity: Two Full Years (24 Months).
  • Testing domains: Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning, and Analytical Reasoning.

Graduate Assessment Test (GAT™ - Subject)

The GAT™ - Subject is a compulsory examination for candidates pursuing admission into Ph.D. degree programs nationwide. It assesses advanced subject-matter expertise across 28 distinct academic disciplines.

Paper Distribution:
  • 70% Subject Specialization: Core theoretical and applied concepts of the specific discipline.
  • 30% General Section: English Verbal (15%) + Analytical Reasoning (15%).
  • Conducted quarterly (4 times a year).

Screening & Recruitment Tests

NTS provides customized, end-to-end recruitment screening solutions for government ministries, provincial departments, defense organizations, banking institutions, and multinational corporations.

Biometric Verification

CNIC verification and biometric thumbprint matching at examination center gates to prevent impersonation.

Multiple Paper Codes

Scrambled color-coded question papers (White, Pink, Green, Yellow) to ensure total exam fairness.

Rapid Digital Marking

High-speed Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) scanners produce audited results within 48 to 72 hours.

International Assessments (TOEFL®-ITP & TOEIC®)

NTS is the official authorized country representative for premier international assessments developed by Educational Testing Service (ETS, USA):

  • TOEFL® ITP (Institutional Testing Program): Evaluates non-native English language proficiency for academic study and scholarship selection.
  • TOEIC® (Test of English for International Communication): Global benchmark for workplace English proficiency utilized by multinational employers.
  • WorkFORCE® Assessment: Behavioral and cognitive evaluation for workforce readiness and corporate placement.
