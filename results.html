Paper Rechecking Guidelines

Candidates who wish to apply for paper rechecking of their OMR response sheets may do so within 15 days of the result announcement. Paper rechecking comprises re-scanning of the candidate's original OMR response sheet to ensure no questions were left unscanned or miscalculated.
