National Aptitude Test (NAT™-I) — Undergraduate Admissions

The NAT-I test is designed for candidates with 12 years of education. Total Questions: 90 MCQs | Total Time: 120 Minutes.

Category / Group Verbal (English) Analytical Reasoning Quantitative Subject Portion Total MCQs
NAT-IE (Pre-Engineering) 20 MCQs 20 MCQs 20 MCQs Physics (10) + Chem (10) + Math (10) = 30 90
NAT-IM (Pre-Medical) 20 MCQs 20 MCQs 20 MCQs Physics (10) + Chem (10) + Biology (10) = 30 90
NAT-ICS (Computer Science) 20 MCQs 20 MCQs 20 MCQs Physics (10) + Computer (10) + Math (10) = 30 90
NAT-ICOM (Commerce) 20 MCQs 20 MCQs 20 MCQs Accounting (10) + Commerce (10) + Economics (10) = 30 90
NAT-IA (Arts / Humanities) 20 MCQs 20 MCQs 20 MCQs Pak Studies (10) + Islamiyat (10) + GK (10) = 30 90

Graduate Assessment Test (GAT™ - General) — MS/M.Phil Admissions

The GAT-General test evaluates candidate suitability for higher research degree programs. Total Questions: 100 MCQs | Total Time: 120 Minutes.

Test Category Verbal Reasoning Quantitative Reasoning Analytical Reasoning Total MCQs
GAT-A (Business & Engineering) 35% (35 Questions) 35% (35 Questions) 30% (30 Questions) 100
GAT-B (Arts, Humanities & Social Sciences) 50% (50 Questions) 25% (25 Questions) 25% (25 Questions) 100
GAT-C (Agricultural, Biological & Medical) 40% (40 Questions) 30% (30 Questions) 30% (30 Questions) 100
GAT-D (Religious Studies / Dars-e-Nizami) 50% (50 Questions) 20% (20 Questions) 30% (30 Questions) 100

Guidelines for Shading OMR Response Sheets

1. Only use Blue or Black ballpoint pens. Markers, gel pens, fountain pens, and pencils are strictly disallowed.

2. Fill the complete circle/bubble corresponding to your chosen option. Incomplete filling, ticks (✓), or crosses (✗) will not be read by optical scanners.

3. Do not fold, staple, or tear your answer sheet.

4. Ensure you take your pink/yellow carbon copy of the answer sheet with you after the exam to verify your score against official answer keys uploaded on nts.org.pk.
