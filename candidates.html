Please select your test project and enter your 13-digit CNIC (with or without dashes) to view and print your official Roll Number Slip.

Important Instructions for Test Day

  • Candidates must reach the test venue at least 45 minutes before the reporting time. Center gates will be closed 30 minutes prior to exam start.
  • Bring this printed Roll Number Slip along with your Original CNIC / Original Smart Card / Passport. Photocopies or digital copies are strictly unacceptable.
  • Mobile phones, smart watches, calculators, bags, and electronic pens are strictly prohibited inside examination centers.
  • Use only Blue or Black ballpoint pens. Markers and pencils are not permitted on OMR answer sheets.
