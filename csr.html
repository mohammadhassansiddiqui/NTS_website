100% Fee Exemption for Special Persons (Persons with Disabilities)

As part of NTS’s steadfast commitment to social equity and the directives of the Government of Pakistan, all candidates with disabilities are completely exempt from NTS test fees (Zero Fee Charged) across all academic admission tests (NAT™, GAT™ General, GAT™ Subject) and professional recruitment screening projects.

Eligibility Requirements:

  • Candidate must hold an official CNIC with Disability Monogram / Wheelchair Logo issued by NADRA.
  • OR possess a valid Disability Certificate issued by the National/Provincial Council for the Rehabilitation of Disabled Persons (NCRDP / Social Welfare Department).

Exam Hall Facilities for Special Persons:

  • Ground Floor Seating: Dedicated examination halls located on the ground floor with ramp access for wheelchairs.
  • Scribe Facility: Official helper/writer provided for visually impaired candidates or individuals with motor disabilities upon prior request.
  • Compensatory Extra Time: Additional 20 minutes per hour of examination time provided in accordance with international testing protocols.

Scholarships & Talent Hunting in Remote Regions

NTS actively collaborates with the Higher Education Commission (HEC), provincial education foundations, and donor agencies to conduct free-of-cost screening tests for scholarship programs in remote and underprivileged areas of Pakistan:

  • Balochistan Talent Hunt Program: Dedicated testing centers established across Quetta, Khuzdar, Turbat, Gwadar, and Loralai.
  • Gilgit-Baltistan & AJK Educational Initiatives: Local assessment centers providing access to university entrance examinations without requiring long-distance travel.
  • USAID / Need-Based Scholarships: 100% sponsored aptitude tests for financially disadvantaged meritorious students.
