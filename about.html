Institutional Profile & Background

National Testing Service - Pakistan (NTS™) was established in July 2002 in compliance with the National Education Policy (1998–2010) and the Information Technology Policy of the Government of Pakistan. It was established as a premier self-sustained assessment organization to bring credibility, transparency, and merit-based evaluation to the educational and recruitment systems of the country.

NTS is incorporated as a public sector, non-profit company limited by guarantee under Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2017. The organization is promoted by COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI) and operates under the stewardship of an autonomous Board of Directors comprising eminent educationists, corporate leaders, and senior government administrators.

Over the past 24 years, NTS has conducted standardized tests for more than 22 million candidates across 15,000+ assessment centers nationwide. Its testing systems are trusted by over 1,000 corporate clients, federal ministries, provincial government departments, autonomous authorities, and leading public and private universities.

Core Values

NTS conducts all operations in strict alignment with its five pillars of organizational integrity:

  • Transparency: 100% open scrutiny with carbon copies of answer sheets provided to candidates.
  • Merit: Zero tolerance for nepotism or compromise on evaluation benchmarks.
  • Confidentiality: Multiple paper codes, computerized scrambled question banks, and secure vaults.
  • Innovation: Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) scanners, electronic result generation, and 1Bill digital payments.
  • Inclusiveness: Complete fee waivers for disabled persons and accessible testing centers.
