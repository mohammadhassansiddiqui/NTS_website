    Fee Exemption Policy for Special Persons

    Complete Fee Waiver for Persons with Disabilities

    1. Scope & Eligibility

    Pakistani citizens with a valid CNIC carrying the disability monogram or disability certificate issued by the Social Welfare Department are 100% exempt from the NTS test fee.

    2. Procedure

    Select "Person with Disability" while creating your profile on portal.nts.org.pk and upload your CNIC/certificate. Test fee will automatically be waived.
